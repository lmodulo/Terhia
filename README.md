## Terhia
Terhia is a shared, open-source fantasy canon and world-building framework designed for multi-media deployment across novellas, graphic novels, tabletop roleplaying frameworks, and video games.
Unlike traditional "kitchen-sink" fantasy settings, Terhia operates as a clockwork engine where cosmos, culture, magic, and mechanics are rigidly locked into an immutable mathematical grid.

## 🏗️ The System Architecture
The world engine is built upon a base-five cosmological and mechanical matrix:

* The Calendar & Moons: Years are exactly 400 days long, divided into five distinct lunar phases lasting 80 days each: Velquor (Black) $\rightarrow$ Saelura (Red) $\rightarrow$ Orrivane (Purple) $\rightarrow$ Namaris (Gold) $\rightarrow$ Threnis (Green).
* The Symmetrical Law of Cost: Magic and martial abilities do not use a standard "mana pool." The cost of invoking power is a literal, symmetrical, and visceral degradation of the user's human experience (fatigue, memory dissonance, touch erosion, or lifespan compression).
* Ethos vs. Institution: Ethos represents personal belief and character class (Rationalist, Devout, Reverie, Ethosless), while Institution represents the transnational governing or licensing charter (Blue Accord, Gold Crown, Green Chimera). These axes operate independently, creating realistic geopolitical and socioeconomic friction.
* The Ecological Engine: All creatures and environmental anomalies are algorithmically produced, not designed, using a rigid compositional formula:
$$\text{Creature} = \text{Base Animal} \times \text{Biome} \times \text{Moon} \times \text{Ethos}$$

## 📂 Project Repository Directory

* Canon.md — The core system data detailing the Moons, Magic Schools, Martial Disciplines, Alchemy rules, and their respective physical and emotional cost progressions.
* Canon-culture.md — Detailed breakdowns of Terhia's six core cultural groups (Menneske, Renlei, Unsaan, Yamana, Umutu, and Tangata) and the structural asymmetries caused by their alignments with institutional charters.
* Canon-history-1145-1245.md — A survey of the historic century (1145–1245), providing a complete, interlocking timeline of the geopolitical events, treaties, purges, and substrate anomalies driving the ongoing narrative.
* Canon-visual-grammar.md — The graphic novel style-guide mapping narrative tension directly onto visual panel compression scales and school-specific aesthetic palettes.

## ⚖️ Open-Source Licensing & Contribution Guidelines
Terhia operates on a Locked Core / Open Module architecture:

   1. The Kernel is Closed: Contributors may not alter or add to the core structural framework (e.g., adding a sixth moon, modifying the magic schools, or introducing a new baseline culture is prohibited). The mathematical engine must remain balanced to maintain cross-media interoperability.
   2. The Modules are Open: You are invited to build on top of the Terhia kernel. We welcome communal world-building contributions in the form of custom settlements, regional historical conflicts, regional trade tariffs, specific beast configurations using the generation API, and individual character episodes.

For specific legal terms regarding Creative Commons usage, attribution standards, and module submission pipelines, please refer to the licensing documentation in this repository.
