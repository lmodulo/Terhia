# VISUAL GRAMMAR (GRAPHIC NOVEL ADAPTATION)

## Purpose

Visual language specification for graphic novel adaptation. Panel layout, density, and composition encode the same escalation mechanics that prose encodes through sentence length and scene compression.

---

## Panel Compression Scale

Panel density is a direct analog of narrative tension.

| Narrative Stage | Layout Density | Visual Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Stable | 6-panel grid | Standard pacing. Room to breathe. Environmental detail visible |
| Rising | Narrow gutters | Panels press together. White space decreases. Reader acceleration begins |
| Crisis | Tight compression | Panels overlap or crowd. Gutters nearly gone. Claustrophobic density |
| Guru Event | Full bleed (max 2 per volume) | Panel boundary eliminated. Image fills the page. Reserved for irreversible moments only |

**Constraint:** Full bleed pages — maximum two per volume. Overuse collapses the hierarchy. The restriction ensures that when a boundary breaks visually, the reader feels it.

---

## Correspondence to Narrative Architecture

| Narrative Element | Visual Analog |
|---|---|
| Act I (space to observe) | 6-panel standard. Wide establishing shots |
| Act II (interference) | Narrow gutters. Panels begin to intrude on each other |
| Act III (no space) | Tight compression. Pressed to panel edges |
| Uncontrollable event | Panel disruption — broken border, bleed, or layout violation |
| Institutional failure | Rigid grid imposed on chaotic content (form contradicts content) |
| Crossing sequence | Full bleed |
| Aftermath | Return to 6-panel, but with visible damage — cracked gutters, imperfect alignment |

---

## POV Encoding

Each POV carries a distinct visual signature beyond character design:

| Persona Attribute | Visual Expression |
|---|---|
| Epistemic lens | What the panels *show* (Arcane POV: overlay geometry. Nature POV: body detail) |
| Abstraction level | Panel framing distance (concrete = close-up; abstract = wide/diagrammatic) |
| Stress response | Panel rhythm changes (shortened sentences = rapid small panels; silence = empty panel or gutter expansion) |
| Metaphor domain | Background detail and environmental texture |

---

## School-Specific Visual Language

| School | Visual Signature | Color Profile |
|---|---|---|
| Arcane | Geometric overlays, mathematical notation visible in environment | Cool blues, silver, white |
| Elemental | Environmental distortion, heat shimmer, particle effects | Warm spectrum — flame, stone, water, wind coded |
| Divine | Clean light, symmetrical composition, wards as visible geometry | Gold, warm white |
| Necrotic | Boundary edges visible as physical lines, death as texture | Grey, muted, edges sharp |
| Nature | Organic detail, body interior visible through touch, channels as flowing paths | Green-gold, warm earth |

---

## Adaptation Rules

1. Panel compression must track narrative escalation. A visually relaxed crisis page is a structural error.
2. Full bleed is irreversible. Once used, the narrative has crossed a threshold. The visual cannot retreat to stable grid without marking the cost.
3. The least abstract perceiver gets the visual climax. Final crossing sequence rendered through the most concrete visual language available.
4. Institutional documents appear as text panels — rendered in institutional typography, not hand-lettered. The visual distinction between lived experience (drawn) and institutional record (typeset) must be maintained.
5. No single panel contains the whole truth. Perceptual fragmentation applies to visual storytelling. Different POVs seeing the same event produce visually distinct panels.
